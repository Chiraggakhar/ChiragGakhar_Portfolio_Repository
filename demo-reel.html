Inside the Work

This demo reel presents a selection of my work in environment art, 3D modelling, texturing and real-time scene development.

The reel highlights my approach to building environments, developing visual detail and presenting scenes through composition, lighting and atmosphere.

Tools: Unreal Engine 5, Autodesk Maya, Autodesk 3ds Max, Substance 3D Painter.

My contribution: Environment creation, 3D modelling, texturing, scene composition and visual presentation.
