01 / WORLDS & LEVELS

Environment Art

Environments, lighting, composition, and real-time worlds.

PROJECT 01

Murder at the laundromat

A dark, atmospheric laundromat transformed into a crime scene, featuring blood-stained washing machines, disturbing wall markings, and dramatic contrasting cyan and red lighting. Modelled and textured using Autodesk Maya and Adobe Substance 3D Painter, and brought to life in Unreal Engine 5 through environmental storytelling, cinematic lighting, and detailed material work.

Unreal Engine 5 · Maya · Substance Painter

PROJECT 02

The Holy Shrine

A mystical shrine set within an overgrown landscape, featuring an ancient ceremonial structure surrounded by wild vegetation and a weathered stone pathway. The environment was modelled and textured using Autodesk Maya and Adobe Substance 3D Painter, then brought to life in Unreal Engine 5, with atmospheric lighting, environmental details, and cinematic composition.

Unreal Engine 5 · Environment Art · Maya

PROJECT 03

The Horror Door

A dark, atmospheric horror environment featuring a heavily chained and barricaded door, creating a sense of mystery, tension, and suspense. The scene focuses on detailed textures, dramatic red lighting, and cinematic composition to establish an unsettling atmosphere.

Unreal Engine 5 · Level Design

PROJECT 04

Crash Site

One of my most extensive environment art projects, featuring a crashed aircraft hidden within a vast desert canyon. The scene focuses on creating an immersive, believable environment through detailed rock formations, natural vegetation, environmental storytelling, and carefully crafted lighting. The abandoned aircraft and makeshift structures bring the scene to life, suggesting a story of survival and isolation.

Environment Art · Lighting · Composition

02 / MODELLING & TEXTURING

Game Art

A closer look at my modelling, UVs, texturing, materials, and finished 3D assets.

ASSET 01

The Radio

A 3D model of a vintage-style radio, designed with a focus on clean geometry, balanced proportions, and realistic material details. The project explores the combination of aged wooden finishes, textured surfaces, and metallic controls to capture a classic retro aesthetic.

Breakdown

  • Modelling: Autodesk Maya / 3ds Max
  • Texturing: Adobe Substance 3D Painter
  • UVs, wireframe, and texture maps

ASSET 02

Skateboard

A 3D skateboard model showcasing a combination of realistic materials and vibrant graphic design. The project explores the individual components of a skateboard, from the wooden deck and textured grip tape to the trucks and wheels. Special attention was given to surface details, material variation, and the custom underside artwork to achieve a realistic and visually appealing finish.

Breakdown

  • Modelling: Autodesk Maya / 3ds Max
  • Texturing: Adobe Substance 3D Painter
  • Show additional renders, UVs, and texture maps
