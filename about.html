THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

About Me.

I'm Chirag Gakhar, an Environment Artist with a passion for building immersive spaces and visually compelling interactive worlds.

My Background

My background in game design has helped me develop an understanding of how environments, composition, lighting and spatial design contribute to the player experience. I enjoy combining artistic experimentation with practical problem-solving to create detailed and believable spaces.

Through academic projects, collaborative game development and personal work, I have gained hands-on experience in environment creation, 3D modelling, texturing, level design and presenting work in Unreal Engine.

I am particularly interested in creating atmospheric environments where visual details and the layout of a space contribute to the overall experience.
